# Awesome-Site-Management-Organization-Software

## Top Site Management Organization (SMO) / Clinical Site Software Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Clinical Trial Site Operations, CTMS, eISF/eBinders, Regulatory Document Management, Visit Tracking & Site Financials*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Site Management Organization (SMO)** and **clinical research site software**. These tools help research sites, site networks, and SMOs manage studies, participants, regulatory documents (eISF), visits, staffing, budgets, and compliance.



**Examples** include RealTime CTMS, Clinical Conductor (Advarra), Florence eBinders, Advarra CTMS / OnCore, SimpleTrials, Complion, Veeva SiteVault, Trial Interactive, CRIO, and EDGE CTMS (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Fully featured commercial-grade site CTMS and eISF platforms are predominantly SaaS. Open-source options are more limited and often oriented toward academic research, data management, or broader clinical trial frameworks. **Phoenix CTMS**, **clinicedc**, LibreClinica/OpenClinica community editions, and related projects provide the strongest available foundations. This section lists every significant relevant project found.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[RealTime CTMS](https://realtime-ctms.com/)**  

  Clinical trial management system built specifically for research sites and site networks, covering recruitment, scheduling, visit tracking, and site financial workflows.



- **[Clinical Conductor / Advarra CTMS](https://www.advarra.com/)**  

  Site- and academic-focused CTMS solutions (including Clinical Conductor and OnCore) for protocol management, subjects, staffing, financials, and research operations.



- **[Florence eBinders](https://www.florencehc.com/)**  

  Leading electronic investigator site file (eISF) / eRegulatory platform used by thousands of research teams for document management and inspection readiness.



- **[Veeva SiteVault](https://www.veeva.com/)**  

  Site-centric platform from Veeva that includes eISF capabilities and SiteVault CTMS for protocol, visit, and financial management, connected to the broader Veeva clinical ecosystem.



- **[SimpleTrials](https://www.simpletrials.com/)**  

  Accessible CTMS designed for smaller research organizations and sites needing core study and site management features.



- **[Complion](https://www.complion.com/)**  

  eRegulatory and compliance platform focused on investigator site file management and audit readiness.



- **[Trial Interactive, CRIO, EDGE CTMS](https://www.trialinteractive.com/)**  

  Additional site and trial management solutions covering document management, site operations, and clinical research workflows.



- **[Other site & eISF platforms](https://www.advarra.com/)**  

  Tools from sponsors, CROs, and specialists that support site activation, monitoring, payments, and regulatory document exchange.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Phoenix CTMS](https://github.com/phoenixctms/ctsms)**  

  Comprehensive open-source CTMS/PRS/CDMS platform combining patient recruitment, clinical trial management, and clinical data management capabilities. Designed for academic research, CROs, and hospitals.



- **[clinicedc](https://github.com/clinicedc/clinicedc)**  

  Django-based open-source framework for building multisite longitudinal clinical trial data management and eSource systems.



- **[LibreClinica / OpenClinica community](https://github.com/reliatec-gmbh/LibreClinica)**  

  Community-driven open-source clinical trial software for electronic data capture (EDC) and clinical data management, descended from OpenClinica.



- **[RPB (Radiotherapy CTMS platform)](https://github.com/ddRPB/rpb)**  

  Open-source platform supporting clinical trial management with extensions for medical imaging and radiotherapy research data.



- **[QAtrial](https://github.com/MeyerThorsten/QAtrial)**  

  Open-source quality and regulated-workspace platform that includes eTMF, eConsent, CAPA, and related GxP capabilities useful for clinical research quality processes.



- **[ECS – Ethics Committee System](https://github.com/ecs-org/ecs)**  

  Open-source system supporting ethics committee workflows, clinical trial approval, and related regulatory processes.



- **[OpenEMR](https://github.com/openemr/openemr)**  

  Widely used open-source EHR and practice management system that some research sites adapt for scheduling, documentation, and basic research workflows.



- **[Other clinical research tools](https://github.com/search?q=CTMS+OR+%22clinical+trial%22+OR+eISF+OR+eTMF+open+source)**  

  Academic and community projects for randomization, eCRF, subject tracking, and trial administration.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **REDCap**: Widely adopted (often free for academic use) electronic data capture platform frequently used alongside site operations tools.

- **EDC & eSource frameworks**: Additional open-source data collection systems that complement site CTMS functions.

- **Document management**: General open-source DMS tools adapted for regulatory binders and eISF-like workflows.

- **Scheduling & calendar systems**: Open-source tools used for visit and resource scheduling at sites.

- **Audit trail & compliance libraries**: Components that support 21 CFR Part 11-style controls in custom builds.

- Integration layers connecting open-source EDC with site financial or staffing systems.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

For the closest open-source CTMS experience, evaluate **Phoenix CTMS** and frameworks such as **clinicedc**.  

**LibreClinica** and related EDC projects address data capture needs.  

True end-to-end site operations platforms (especially eISF + financials + multi-study site networks) remain dominated by commercial solutions such as RealTime, Advarra, Florence, and Veeva SiteVault because of regulatory validation, support, and site-specific workflows.  

Open-source tools are most valuable for academic centers, custom research units, or organizations that can invest in validation and integration effort.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Clinical research systems handle protected health information and must comply with regulations such as ICH-GCP, 21 CFR Part 11, HIPAA, GDPR, and applicable local requirements. Any system used in regulated trials requires proper validation, audit trails, access controls, and procedural controls.

- Open-source software does not automatically meet regulatory expectations. Organizations remain responsible for system validation, security, and compliance when deploying open-source or custom solutions in clinical research.
